## 🥊 The 3 Core Devil's Advocate Challenges (To Dissect Tomorrow)

### 1. The Referrer's True Incentive (Risk vs. Reputation)
* **The Common Myth:** Job seekers believe employees are motivated by the $2,000–$5,000 referral bonus.
* **The Hard Reality:** For mid-to-senior professionals earning $150k–$400k+, that bonus is secondary. What they protect is their **professional reputation**. Referring an unqualified, pushy, or toxic candidate creates an internal social penalty with recruiters and hiring managers.
* **The Product Question:** How does our outreach prove *competence and trustworthiness* rather than just polite copywriting? Can text alone bridge the credibility gap?

### 2. The "AI Inundation" Paradox (Manufactured Sincerity)
* **The Hard Reality:** When outreach tools make drafting effortless, inboxes get flooded with generic "hyper-personalized" notes. Professionals quickly develop an instinct for detecting LLM-generated flattery and formulaic hooks.
* **The Product Question:** Where is the boundary between *AI assistance* and *AI spam*? How do we ensure ReferralCraft makes candidates sound authentically human and peer-to-peer, rather than creating uncanny-valley marketing pitches?

### 3. Form Factor & The ChatGPT Threat (Defensibility & Friction)
* **The Hard Reality:** A standalone web app requires 5–6 copy-paste steps across browser tabs (LinkedIn $\rightarrow$ App $\rightarrow$ LinkedIn).
* **The Product Question:** Why would a job seeker use our app repeatedly instead of pasting a prompt into ChatGPT or Claude, which they already keep open? What is our **proprietary, defensible wedge**?
